PLVista

College Capstone Unity 3Ds Max

A 3D virtual tour game where you explore the world of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela. Visitors can walk the Maysan and Annex campuses, find their way with a built-in guide, and unlock avatar cosmetics along the way.

Walkthrough

  1. Users log in to identify as a Guest or a PLV Student.
  2. They choose a venue: Maysan or Annex.
  3. A tutorial then introduces the game's interface and controls.

Features

  • Control compatibility: built for kiosk, mobile and computer, with touch controls or mouse and keyboard.
  • Navigation rewards: walking through the whole campus unlocks cosmetics for your avatar.
  • Wayfinder: pick a destination and the game guides you there.
  • PWD guide: a feature made to guide people with disabilities around campus.
  • Feedback: an in-game form that sends player feedback to the developers.

Limbo: Oblivion Survivor

Recently developed 2.5D Web Game

A 2.5D web game with infinite progression. You defeat enemies, collect the experience they drop and level up. Each level offers three augments based on your weapon, and every 10th level offers a special augment.

How it plays

  • Kill enemies and pick up the experience they drop.
  • Level up to choose from three basic augments that fit your weapon, such as Fire, Lightning and Ice turrets.
  • Reach every 10th level to choose a special augment.

Features

  • Weapons: start with one of three: staff, sword or grimoire.
  • Chests: earn chests by playing and hitting milestones. Each holds a random weapon of a random tier.
  • Weapon merging: with more than 3 copies of the same weapon at the same tier, merge them into an upgraded version.
  • Feedback: an in-game form that sends player feedback to the developers.
